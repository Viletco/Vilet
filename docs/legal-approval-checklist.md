# Legal approval checklist

This is an operational review list, not legal advice.

- [ ] Authorized reviewer has read the complete public privacy page.
- [ ] Operator identity (“Vilét”) is accurate and sufficient.
- [ ] A public privacy contact method is explicitly approved.
- [ ] Contact data collected, purpose, retention language, recipients/processors, and user rights accurately reflect real operations.
- [ ] Optional website, budget, and timeline fields are described accurately.
- [ ] The absence of a phone field and the email/video-call preferences are accurate.
- [ ] Named contact method is real and approved; no placeholder address is published.
- [ ] The active email-delivery provider and receiving-mailbox retention behavior are accurately disclosed.
- [ ] The active rate-limit provider and short-lived identifier wording are accurate.
- [ ] The AI provider, retention, and training settings are reviewed before public activation.
- [ ] Website Analyzer behavior and authorization wording are reviewed before activation.
- [ ] Analytics/cookies are absent unless separately approved and disclosed.
- [ ] Data-retention wording matches actual business and provider practices.
- [ ] International processing and whether a public disclosure is required are reviewed.
- [ ] Children’s privacy wording is approved.
- [ ] Applicable state or other privacy rights are identified without unsupported compliance claims.
- [ ] Reviewer decides whether Terms, cookie notice, consent controls, or jurisdiction-specific disclosures are required.
- [ ] The effective date and last-updated date are accurate.
- [ ] Any required legal page is supplied or approved by qualified counsel before implementation.
- [ ] Final legal approval is recorded with reviewer and date in the owner worksheet/decision registry without confidential advice.

The current site must not invent a legal contact, company registration, address, certification, or policy date.
